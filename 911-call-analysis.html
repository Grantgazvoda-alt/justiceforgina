SOURCE-COMPARISON QUESTIONS

The recording can test statements—not read minds.

A reliable review timestamps exact words and compares them with dispatch, police, EMS, OCME, phone, location, witness, and later testimony records.

PRESENCE

Who was present?

Do not assign an unidentified background voice to a person without independent corroboration.

INSTRUCTIONS

What was requested and reported?

Separate dispatcher instructions, caller statements, audible activity, EMS documentation, and later witness accounts.

CHRONOLOGY

What earlier movements were described?

Compare statements with dispatch timestamps, official narratives, phone and location records, and later sworn testimony.

CHANGES

Do later accounts differ?

Record exact changes and possible explanations. A discrepancy is a proof target, not automatic evidence of guilt.
