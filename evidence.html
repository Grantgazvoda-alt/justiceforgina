HOW TO READ THIS ARCHIVE

Source class comes before conclusion.

A court finding is different from an official-record narrative. Sworn testimony is different from a judicial finding. An expert opinion based on reproductions is different from an original-document examination. A family theory remains attributed and unproven unless independent evidence establishes it.

The current controlled record supports

  • A Probate Court finding that the original July 28, 2019 will was fraudulent and invalid.
  • A Superior Court order granting Maiorano Funeral Home summary judgment on counts 5–7 while counts 1–4 remained pending.
  • Documented gaps and conflicts in death-certificate provenance, medical support, authority records, official-response accounts, and productions.
  • No documented OCME autopsy, toxicology, scene examination, or independent injury assessment in the controlled file.

The current controlled record does not establish

  • Who caused Gina's death or that homicide has been proven through competent forensic evidence.
  • Poisoning, a specific toxic agent, dose, route, timing, or responsible actor.
  • Illegal cremation, theft, obstruction, perjury, document alteration by a named person, or guilt on pending charges.
  • That an absent family copy never existed or was intentionally concealed or destroyed.
