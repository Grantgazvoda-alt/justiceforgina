REPORTING CAUTIONS

Distinctions that must remain clear.

Accurate reporting preserves both the family's credibility and the rights of every person named in the record.

COURT

State exactly what was decided

Do not extend a court finding to cause of death, every disputed document, another party, or uncharged conduct.

OFFICIAL RECORD

Distinguish narrative from finding

An intake statement, police narrative, certificate field, or account entry can be official without independently proving the disputed fact inside it.

EXPERT

State source-generation limits

Reproduction-based document concerns do not identify an actor or substitute for original-document examination.

FAMILY

Attribute theories and witness accounts

Possible toxic exposure and other family hypotheses remain unproven without competent independent evidence.

Do not publish: private addresses, unnecessary medical details, account data, signatures, source-device identifiers, witness contact information, privileged material, or unsupported allegations of homicide, poisoning, theft, forgery, obstruction, perjury, or illegal cremation.
