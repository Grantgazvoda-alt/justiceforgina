DEATH CERTIFICATE · PRONOUNCER–CERTIFIER CONFLICT

The certificate fields and Panwar's sworn testimony require a complete version and audit review.

Dr. Nirmala Panwar testified that she did not see Gina's body. The death certificate identifies her as both pronouncer and medical certifier. That conflict is material under the governing pronouncement and certification framework, but the public record is incomplete and no final violation finding is asserted here.

GOVERNING FRAMEWORK

Pronouncement and medical certification are distinct functions.

The 2021 Connecticut framework identified personal-viewing requirements and allowed a later medical certifier to differ from the person who made the determination and pronouncement. Applying that law to a disputed registry entry requires the complete certificate versions, field history, and qualified legal or agency review.
