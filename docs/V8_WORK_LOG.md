## Documentation protocol

Every significant action should record:

1. Date and operator role.
2. Source universe reviewed.
3. Exact file, record, route, branch, or commit affected.
4. Stable issue or claim identifier where applicable.
5. Evidentiary classification.
6. What changed and why.
7. Validation performed.
8. Remaining limitation or competing explanation.
9. Approval status.
10. Next action.

Source-controlled conclusions must not be silently overwritten. New material must be marked as confirming, narrowing, contradicting, or superseding earlier work.

## Approval gates

Explicit approval remains required before:

- production deployment or merge;
- live DNS or domain changes;
- external communications, filings, submissions, or witness contact;
- public release of sensitive allegations or private records;
- spending or paid-service activation;
- destructive deletion, history rewrite, force push, or irreversible migration.
