Disposition, cremation authority, documentation, and timing

Nicholas signed broad authority representations. An apparent § 20-230c-type disposition page is present, but complete execution and original authentication remain unresolved. The recorded waiting interval appears compliant.

What is documented

  • Nicholas represented that he was next of kin, had custody and control, possessed full legal authority, and knew of no objection.

  • Illegal cremation or knowing disregard of a proven objection.

CONTROLLED CONCLUSION

Cause and manner of death

The present controlled record does not establish who caused Gina's death or prove homicide through competent forensic evidence.

What can be said

  • The certified causal chain has documented source and support questions.
  • No autopsy, toxicology, or independent OCME examination is documented in the controlled file.
  • Cremation eliminated the opportunity for a later conventional autopsy and toxicology examination.
  • The family may ask investigators and experts to examine traumatic, toxic, medication-related, accidental, and natural alternatives.

What cannot be said as established

  • That Gina was murdered.
  • That Gina was poisoned.
  • A specific substance, route, dose, timing, or responsible actor.
  • That every natural cause was absent.
  • That a named person caused Gina's death.
Lost-opportunity limit: The absence of competent postmortem testing leaves material alternatives unresolved; it does not prove one preferred theory.
