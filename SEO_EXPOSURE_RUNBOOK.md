Make Justice for Gina easy for Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, other search engines, journalists, attorneys, experts, and supporters to discover for accurate branded searches concerning Gina Marie Gazvoda.

## DuckDuckGo and other engines

DuckDuckGo maintains its own crawler and indexes and also sources many traditional results from Bing. Strong Bing discovery, clean crawl access, useful content, consistent identity, and reputable external references therefore improve the site's opportunity to appear there.

IndexNow submissions are shared among participating search engines, but acceptance only confirms receipt. It does not guarantee crawling, indexing, placement, or rank.

## Authority and external exposure

Prioritize accurate, editorially earned references rather than mass link submissions:

- Use the exact website URL and `#JusticeForGina` on the official TikTok profile and posts.
- Keep the publisher name, project name, subject name, and website URL consistent on GoFundMe and other authorized public profiles.
- Provide journalists and qualified reviewers with the press page and source packet rather than unsupported summaries.
- Seek lawful links from relevant news coverage, attorney or expert publications, victim-advocacy resources, public-record discussions, and nonprofit partners.
- Publish substantive updates when a new primary source, court event, verified records response, expert report, or correction is available.
- Link every update to the relevant evidence, timeline, case-status, standards, or funding page.

Do not buy backlinks, automate comment spam, create doorway pages, clone thin pages, hide keyword text, or publish sensational unsupported allegations. Those tactics damage credibility and may reduce search visibility.

## Content cadence

For every material update:

1. Verify the source and classification.
2. Update the controlling page.
3. Update the page's visible revision date and structured-data `dateModified` value.
4. Update `sitemap.xml` only with the true modification date.
5. Add internal links from the most relevant existing pages.
6. Deploy and confirm HTTP 200, canonical URL, title, description, schema, mobile layout, and no broken links.
7. Let the IndexNow workflow submit the canonical URLs.
8. Request Google re-indexing only for important changes, not routine cosmetic edits.
9. Share the exact page through the official TikTok account using `#JusticeForGina` and source-accurate language.
