## Triple-check protocol

Every material V8 statement must pass three separate reviews before it is shown to Grant for approval:

### Pass 1 — Source verification

- Confirm the actual source exists and is the correct version.
- Record exact page, line, paragraph, docket, file ID, date, and originator where available.
- Confirm whether the source is official, sworn, expert, witness-attributed, family-created, or unresolved.

### Pass 2 — Contradiction and limitation review

- Search for conflicting records, corrected testimony, alternative explanations, copied narratives, and missing context.
- State what the evidence proves and what it does not prove.
- Remove any implication of motive, knowledge, causation, forgery, theft, poisoning, or homicide that is not independently established.

### Pass 3 — Publication and technical review

- Check privacy, privilege, redaction, legal framing, accessibility, broken links, public permissions, metadata exposure, and mobile rendering.
- Confirm every allegation is attributed and every pending charge retains the presumption-of-innocence notice.
- Confirm no restricted source is made public merely because it is indexed internally.
