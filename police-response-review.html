It does not presently contain November 21 scene photographs, a scene diagram, a complete evidence/property record, a body-camera file or no-record certification, officer notes, or a supplemental investigative narrative sufficient to reconstruct the scene response.

What is located and not located

This classification describes the connected archive and produced materials. It does not certify what was or was not created in agency systems.

Located

  • Minimal police incident report.

Not located in the controlled production

WHAT THE GAP MEANS

Independent reconstruction is limited.

Without the complete native record, later reviewers cannot determine from the family archive alone what officers observed, who was present, what was moved, what statements were made, what evidence was considered, or whether additional records exist elsewhere.

Evidence boundary: The present production gap does not prove a body-camera, scene-documentation, evidence-preservation, or supervisory policy violation. The controlling November 2021 Waterbury policies, officer equipment assignments, audit records, and complete production are still needed.
UNRESOLVED POLICY QUESTIONS

Six questions require an agency or custodian answer.

The questions are evidence targets, not accusations.

01Were responding officers equipped with body cameras in November 2021?
02What policies governed activation, death scenes, photographs, notes, and supplemental reports?
03Were photographs, recordings, CAD attachments, officer notes, or evidence records created?
04What was communicated to OCME, by whom, and when?
05Who was present when police arrived, and where are contemporaneous statements?
06What explains the unresolved 17:15 narrative entry?
