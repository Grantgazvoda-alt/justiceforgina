WITNESSES · AUDIO · VIDEO · PHOTOGRAPHS

Preserved media and witness accounts must be separated by source, generation, and personal knowledge.

The archive includes official-source audio, family-supplied media or descriptions, expert working material, and witness leads. No derivative recording, screenshot, recollection, or preliminary review should be treated as equivalent to a native original or qualified forensic conclusion.

MEDIA CONTROL

What is presently supportable

The official-source 911 audio is preserved and publicly reviewed with responsible-use limits. Other media and witness materials remain subject to source authentication, privacy, custody, and expert review.

Controlled observations

  • The official-source 911 recording can be used for exact statement and timing comparison.
  • A preliminary forensic media/pathology working report was created for restricted review.
  • Family and witness accounts identify potential factual leads that can be tested against independent records.
  • Preserved hashes can verify that a specific file did not change after preservation.

What is not established

  • Credibility or guilt from voice tone, emotion, resemblance, or demeanor.
  • Cause or manner of death from photographs or video alone.
  • Authenticity of every screenshot, forwarded clip, compressed file, or transcription.
  • A complete witness account where recollections were discussed or recorded jointly.

WITNESS PROTOCOL

Collect first-person accounts separately and neutrally.

Each statement should distinguish what the witness personally saw or heard, what another person told them, and what they later inferred. Formal contact, affidavits, subpoenas, or recorded interviews remain counsel- and approval-controlled.

PERSONAL KNOWLEDGE

Direct observations

Record date, time, location, sensory observation, certainty, and any contemporaneous note or message.

HEARSAY LEADS

What the witness was told

Identify the speaker, approximate time, exact wording if remembered, and whether the statement was recorded or repeated.

INFERENCE

Later interpretation

Keep conclusions, suspicions, and reconstructed meaning separate from the original observation.

CONTAMINATION CONTROL

No coordinated recollections

Preserve original drafts and avoid sharing one witness's account with another before independent collection.

MEDIA INTAKE

Preserve the file before analyzing it.

For each item, record filename, size, format, source device, creation and modification times, transfer path, acquisition date, hash, compression history, cropping, transcoding, and whether the item is original, copy, screenshot, export, or derivative.

Expert-required boundary: Injury interpretation, postmortem change, toxicology, cause and manner of death, voice identification, manipulation detection, and document authenticity require the relevant qualified expert and appropriate source material.

NEXT DISCRIMINATING STEP

Secure native media and separate witness statements under a documented protocol.

Every new item should receive a stable evidence ID, preservation hash, custody entry, privacy status, and relationship to the claim and chronology matrices.
