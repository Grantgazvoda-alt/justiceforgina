OFFICIAL RECORD COMPARISON

The response records preserve materially different accounts of the same critical window.

Police recorded that Nicholas Forte left at about 6:00 a.m. “for work” and returned around 7:00 p.m. EMS recorded that he left to go fishing. The family reports that he repeatedly described fishing with his son. OCME later used wording referring to fishing charter boats in Niantic.

Limit: These differences are documented discrepancies. They do not by themselves establish that a person knowingly lied, where anyone actually was, or who caused Gina's death.

Police narrative

  • Departure at approximately 6:00 a.m.
  • Purpose described as work.
  • Return described at approximately 7:00 p.m.

EMS narrative

  • Departure described as going fishing.
  • Attempted calls referenced around 5:30 and 6:30 p.m.
  • Patient contact and response events occur later in the evening.

Still unresolved

  • Original speaker and exact wording supplied to each responder.
  • Whether “work,” “fishing,” and “charter boats” referred to overlapping activity.
  • Native phone, vehicle, toll, marina, office-access, computer, and surveillance evidence.
  • Independent statement from Nicholas Jr.
