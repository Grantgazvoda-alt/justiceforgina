SWORN TESTIMONY

Key source-locked findings

Testimony located

  • Kate said she had not reviewed the production before deposition because she lacked time, while also stating she supplied Gina’s entire file and, as far as she knew, all materials.
  • She testified no original directive remained in her file and that the original went to Gina.
  • She confirmed the November 22, 2021 email transmitting “Health Care Directive Gina.pdf,” but did not remember who requested it or sending the 7:12 a.m. email.

Unresolved

  • Whether all responsive files were produced.

DOCUMENT-PRODUCTION CONTEXT

Production and testimony must not be conflated.

The 143-page file labeled as produced for Kate’s deposition is a production packet, not the stenographic transcript. It includes multiple document versions, post-death transmissions, probate-preparation materials, and a reference to a missing attachment called “probate bill (3).pdf.”

Safe conclusion: The testimony raises material completeness, custody, memory, and version-control questions. It does not identify who altered any document or prove criminal intent.
