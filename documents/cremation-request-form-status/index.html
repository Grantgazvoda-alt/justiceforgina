STATUTORY REQUIREMENT

What § 20-230c requires

When cremation is requested by the person with custody and control under § 45a-318, the funeral director must complete a written form containing specified information.

Required contents and execution

  • Funeral service business name and address.
  • Deceased person's name.
  • Place and time of cremation.
  • Licensed funeral director or embalmer.
  • Name and address of the person with custody and control.
  • Requested disposition of the cremated remains.
  • Signatures and dates of both the person with custody and control and the funeral director.
  • A copy provided to the person with custody and control.

Retention and legal limits

  • The original signed form must be maintained by the funeral service business for at least six years.
  • The statute identifies required documentation; it does not by itself determine the validity of claimed authority or whether a cremation was unlawful.
  • The legal relationship between separate forms requires the originals, production history, custodian evidence, and qualified legal review.
