The court granted Maiorano Funeral Home summary judgment on counts 5 through 7.

Order No. 442319 in docket NNH-CV23-6138366-S resolved the claims then presented against the funeral home in counts 5 through 7. The order stated that counts 1 through 4 remained pending.

COURT FINDING

What the order decided

The court reviewed the funeral home's summary-judgment motion, the plaintiffs' objection, the reply, oral argument, and the law applied in the order.

What the order establishes

  • The court granted Maiorano Funeral Home's motion for summary judgment on counts 5 through 7.
  • The plaintiffs' objection was overruled.
  • The court concluded, on the record and statutes before it, that the funeral home had no duty to investigate the authenticity of the documents presented for the cremation.
  • The court found no disputed material fact or triable issue as to the funeral home's role in those counts.
  • The order stated that counts 1 through 4 remained pending.

What the order does not establish

  • Gina's cause or manner of death.
  • That every disputed document is authentic.
  • Criminal guilt or liability.
  • The merits or disposition of claims against other defendants.
  • Whether a later appeal, reargument, amended pleading, or legally distinct claim was available or filed.

SOURCE AND PROCEDURAL SCOPE

A ruling about the funeral home's duty is not a universal authenticity finding.

The order must be read within the claims, parties, record, and legal theories actually before the court. Party allegations and special defenses remain litigation positions unless adopted in a controlling ruling.
