CONNECTICUT OCME · OFFICIAL CONTACT RECORD

The file documents a telephone notice and declination—not an independent forensic examination.

The controlled four-page record states that OCME was notified at 7:33 p.m. on November 21, 2021, recorded a 7:15 p.m. home pronouncement, received statements including “nothing suspicious” and “no trauma,” and declined jurisdiction.

Critical distinction: Those phrases were incoming reports. The file does not document an OCME autopsy, external examination, toxicology, scene examination, or independent injury assessment.
