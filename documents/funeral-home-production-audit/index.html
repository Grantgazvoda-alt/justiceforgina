The produced file does not contain a complete native history of intake, source attribution, corrections, and authority.

PRODUCTION AUDIT

What the current production shows

The controlled file contains funeral-home forms, copies, communications, and disposition records sufficient to identify several source and authority questions.

Documented observations

  • Multiple employees communicated with Nicholas Forte.
  • Records use different relationship and authority descriptions.
  • The file includes derivative copies rather than a complete native communication and system history.
  • The original death-certificate version and full correction trail are not present in the controlled production.

What remains unresolved

  • Who supplied each demographic, medical, relationship, and authority field.
  • Whether additional records exist in email, telephone, EDRS, scanner, vendor, archive, or employee systems.
  • Whether any missing item was withheld, lost, destroyed, or never created.

MISSING NATIVE RECORDS

A complete production requires the underlying system trail.

PDF copies and printed forms cannot substitute for native email, call, scanner, audit, and correction records when provenance and field attribution are disputed.

COMMUNICATIONS

Native email and call history

Messages with full headers, attachments, call logs, voicemails, employee notes, and handoff records.

DEATH CERTIFICATE

EDRS and correction history

Every version, field-change history, user identity, timestamp, submission, rejection, correction, and certification event.

DOCUMENT INTAKE

Scanner and source records

Original scan files, scanner/device identifiers, operator information, upload timestamps, indexing descriptions, and source-copy custody.

COMPLETENESS

Custodian certification

Systems searched, date ranges, custodians, retention policies, unavailable categories, and whether responsive records were deleted or migrated.

Evidence limit: Nonproduction is a status fact. It is not, by itself, proof of concealment, spoliation, falsification, or intent.

NEXT DISCRIMINATING STEP

Request a native, custodian-certified production by record category and system.

The request should separately identify email, telephony, EDRS, scanner, intake, accounting, cremation-authority, retention, and archive systems.
