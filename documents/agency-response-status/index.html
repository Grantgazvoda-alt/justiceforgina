CUSTODIAN MAP

Highest-value outside sources

Each custodian controls a different piece of the provenance, timeline, authority, medical, financial, digital, or procedural record.

Government and medical custodians

  • Waterbury Police and Fire/EMS: native CAD, reports, supplements, media, policies, and certifications.
  • Connecticut OCME: complete inquiry, approval, designee, and cremation records.
  • Vital Statistics and EDRS: every certificate version, field change, user, and timestamp.
  • Courts and prosecutors: current dockets, operative pleadings, orders, charging documents, and dispositions.
  • 828 Urgent Care and other providers: contemporaneous clinical and certification support.

Private and litigation custodians

  • Funeral home and crematory: native communications, intake, authority, scanner, EDRS, timing, and retention records.
  • Wells Fargo: exact death-certificate image, submission history, metadata, and retention evidence.
  • Counsel and producing parties: authenticated discovery responses, production histories, native files, and declarations.
  • Witnesses and source devices: separate statements and native digital records under approved protocols.

STATUS LANGUAGE

Every public status must use the right verb.

“Forwarded” is different from “reviewed.” “Program screening” is different from “cause-of-death finding.” “Not located in the connected archive” is different from “does not exist.” “Requested” is different from “refused.”

GUIDANCE

Process information supplied

The agency explained rights, programs, contacts, or tracking tools without deciding the underlying facts.

ROUTED

Message forwarded

Receipt and forwarding are established; review, acceptance, investigation, and decision are not implied.

SCREENED

Program eligibility discussed

The communication reflects the program's criteria and information then available, not an independent forensic conclusion.

PARTIAL

Some responsive material produced

Produced categories and omitted categories are listed separately without inferring intent.

Publication boundary: Raw agency correspondence, attachment files, personal contact information, quoted family assertions, legal strategy, and restricted source records remain outside the public site unless separately approved and redacted.
