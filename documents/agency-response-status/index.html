STATUS LANGUAGE

Every public status must use the right verb.

“Not located in the connected archive” is different from “does not exist.” “Requested” is different from “refused.” “Pending” is different from “overdue.” “Withheld under an exemption” requires the actual response and cited basis.

REQUESTED

Request sent

The request, date, recipient, scope, and delivery evidence are recorded.

PENDING

No complete response yet

The status does not state why the response is incomplete unless the custodian said so.

PARTIAL

Some responsive material produced

Produced categories and omitted categories are listed separately.

NOT LOCATED

Controlled negative search

The searched systems and limits are recorded; nonexistence is not assumed.

Publication boundary: Agency correspondence, legal strategy, private contact information, and restricted source records remain outside the public site unless separately approved and redacted.
