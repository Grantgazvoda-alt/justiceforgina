PUBLICATION AND EVIDENCE STANDARD · V9

Document first. Classify precisely. Preserve every meaningful conflict.

The project does not ask readers to trust a conclusion without seeing its source class, scope, limitations, provenance, and unresolved alternatives. Strong language requires stronger evidence.

CLAIM TAXONOMY

Every material assertion receives a classification.

Classification identifies the source and evidentiary posture. It does not tell readers whom to believe.

COURT FINDING

Adjudicated within a defined scope

State the exact order, issue, date, and limitation. Do not extend a probate finding to cause of death or uncharged conduct.

OFFICIAL RECORD

Government or filed record

Confirms what the record says and when it was created; it does not automatically prove every narrative statement inside it.

SWORN TESTIMONY

Attributed statement under oath

Preserve corrections, memory limits, exhibits, and contradictions. Testimony is not automatically a judicial finding.

EXPERT OPINION

Qualified analysis within methodology

State specialty, materials reviewed, source generation, assumptions, limits, and whether originals were examined.

FAMILY OR WITNESS ACCOUNT

Attributed personal knowledge or lead

Separate direct observation, what the person was told, and later inference.

UNRESOLVED QUESTION

Evidence target, not conclusion

State competing explanations and the record capable of distinguishing them.

PROOF-BOUNDARY RULE

Every public record states both sides of its boundary.

V9 records include “establishes,” “does not establish,” and “records needed” fields.

Required affirmative content

  • Exact source and source class.
  • Verified proposition supported by the source.
  • Event or record date where known.
  • Provenance and acquisition notes.
  • Related claim and chronology identifiers.

Required limiting content

  • What the source cannot establish.
  • Alternative explanations still open.
  • Missing original, metadata, audit, custodian, or expert work.
  • Privacy and publication restrictions.
  • Revision history and pre-release recheck needs.

MISSING-RECORD LANGUAGE

Absence is described by the search actually performed.

A record can be missing from the family archive while existing in another system, vendor, device, law office, backup, or custodian file.

NOT LOCATED

Controlled negative search

Identify the systems, files, dates, and custodians actually searched. Do not say “does not exist.”

REQUESTED

Request sent

Record the recipient, date, scope, delivery evidence, and current response status.

PARTIAL

Some categories produced

List produced and omitted categories separately. A partial production is not complete merely because it contains responsive material.

WITHHELD / UNAVAILABLE

Use the custodian's actual basis

Quote or summarize the stated exemption, retention, destruction, migration, or no-record explanation without adding motive.

Never infer automatically: concealment, destruction, spoliation, obstruction, falsification, or professional misconduct from a missing record or incomplete production.
CHRONOLOGY STANDARD

Four clocks must remain separate.

Timeline errors often arise when occurrence, reporting, data entry, and later recollection are collapsed into one time.

Event timeWhen the underlying act or condition occurred.
Report timeWhen information was communicated to another person or agency.
Record timeWhen a system, filing, email, or document captured the information.
Later accountWhen a witness later recalled or testified about the earlier event.
Conflict rule: Preserve the 17:15 narrative entry beside the 19:06–19:15 response cluster until native CAD, audit history, correction records, or custodian testimony resolves it.

DOCUMENT AND MEDIA AUTHENTICATION

Preserve the highest-generation source before interpretation.

A screenshot, scan, forwarded attachment, PDF copy, MP3 derivative, OCR text, or public summary is not equivalent to a native original.

INTAKE

Record provenance

Filename, size, format, page count, source, custodian, acquisition date, transfer path, and source-generation status.

PRESERVE

Hash the preservation copy

Do not edit the source-lock file. Create separate working copies and document transformations.

COMPARE

Build version genealogy

Map content, signatures, notary pages, filenames, Bates numbers, metadata, first appearance, and custody.

ESCALATE

Use the right expert

Original-document examination, forensic pathology, toxicology, digital forensics, accounting, and voice or media analysis require the appropriate specialty and source material.

Hash limit: A cryptographic hash shows that a specific file did not change after preservation. It does not prove when the underlying document was created, who signed it, or whether it accurately represents a physical original.

SOURCE SUPERSESSION

Better reference—not simply newer file.

A public pointer may change without deleting or devaluing an older record. The replacement must concern the same subject and improve custody, completeness, citation, safety, or usability.

PRESERVE

Older records remain evidence

  • Original, contemporaneous, filed, signed, or authenticated sources may outweigh later summaries.
  • Older versions retain identifiers, dates, custody, and status.
  • No source is deleted merely because a public link changes.
COMPARE

Same-subject test

  • Does the candidate add material information or page-level support?
  • Does it correct a documented error or clarify provenance?
  • Is it a safer public derivative of the same source?
RECORD

Explain every change

  • State why the public reference changed.
  • Identify what happened to the prior version.
  • Preserve conflicts instead of silently merging them.
  • Do not call a source verified merely because it is newer or longer.

PRIVACY

Necessary facts, minimum exposure.

Addresses, account numbers, signatures, minors' information, medical details, private contact data, device identifiers, witness information, and unrelated third-party data are removed or withheld when not necessary to understand the record.

LEGAL CONTEXT

Charges are not convictions.

An arrest, complaint, affidavit, expert opinion, family theory, or charging document does not establish guilt. Time-sensitive docket facts must be refreshed from the official source before release.

FUNDING

Donors do not control conclusions.

Support may fund lawful work, but donor preference cannot change evidence classifications, expert opinions, legal strategy, witness contact, or publication decisions.

CORRECTIONS

The public record can be corrected.

Specific, sourced correction requests are checked against the underlying material. Meaningful changes identify what changed, why, and which earlier version remains preserved.

REVIEW WORKFLOW

From source to publication.

01

Acquire

Record source, date, provenance, custody, generation, and version information.

02

Preserve

Create a source-lock copy, hash it, and record all later transformations.

03

Authenticate

Compare identifiers, context, signatures, filing details, metadata, and related records.

04

Protect

Review privacy, privilege, sealing, witness safety, legal restrictions, and true redaction needs.

05

Classify

Assign source, verification, sensitivity, publication, proof, and limitation fields.

06

Publish and revise

Use the strongest current reference, preserve earlier versions, validate routes, and explain corrections.
