PUBLICATION STANDARD

Document first. Preserve every version. Correct openly.

This project separates verified records, pending allegations, family statements, expert opinions, unresolved questions, and archived working material so readers can evaluate each item in context.

EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION

Every public item receives a status.

Status labels describe what a source can establish, not what readers should conclude.

Court or agency record

Verified source identity

An identifiable court, government, or original-source record. Publication confirms the record exists; it does not prove every allegation contained in it.

Family statement

Attributed account

A statement reflecting the family’s understanding or experience. It is labeled and should not be reported as an independent legal finding.

Under review

Not ready for public reliance

A source that requires authentication, context, comparison, redaction, or legal review before publication or stronger characterization.

Restricted

Preserved, not published

Medical, private, privileged, sealed, draft, or third-party-sensitive material may remain available only to authorized legal or professional reviewers.

SOURCE SUPERSESSION

Better reference, not simply newer file.

The site may replace a public link without deleting or devaluing the earlier record. The replacement must concern the same subject and be demonstrably better for the intended public use.

PRESERVE

Older records remain evidence

  • Original, contemporaneous, filed, signed, or authenticated records may be more important than later summaries.
  • Older versions remain archived with their titles, dates, file identifiers, and known custody.
  • No source is deleted merely because a public link changes.
COMPARE

Same-subject test

  • Does the candidate add material information or better citations?
  • Does it correct a documented error or clarify provenance and limitations?
  • Is it safer for public release or an exact duplicate under better archival control?
RECORD

Explain every change

  • State why the public reference changed.
  • Identify what happened to the prior version.
  • Preserve conflicts instead of silently merging them.
  • Never describe a document as verified merely because it is newer or longer.
Canonical-copy rule: When two files are byte-identical, the site may point to the copy with clearer custody, folder placement, permissions, and access control. That is an archival improvement, not a factual or evidentiary upgrade.

PRIVACY

Necessary facts, minimum exposure.

Personal addresses, account numbers, minors’ information, medical details, private contact information, and unrelated third-party identifiers are removed or withheld whenever publication is not necessary to understand the record.

LEGAL CONTEXT

Charges are not convictions.

Pending allegations are described as allegations. An arrest, complaint, affidavit, expert opinion, or charging document does not establish guilt. Criminal responsibility is determined through lawful proceedings.

CORRECTIONS

The record can be corrected.

Specific, sourced correction requests are reviewed against the underlying material. Meaningful corrections identify what changed, why, and which earlier version remains preserved.

REVIEW WORKFLOW

From source to publication.

01

Acquire

Record the source, date, provenance, custody, and version information available.

02

Authenticate

Compare identifiers, context, signatures, filing information, hashes, and related records.

03

Protect

Review privacy, legal restrictions, privilege, sealing, and true redaction needs.

04

Classify

Assign a clear source, verification, sensitivity, and publication-status label.

05

Publish carefully

Use the strongest current reference, preserve earlier versions, and describe only what the source supports.
