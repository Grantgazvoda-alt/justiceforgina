STATUS VOCABULARY

Use the verb the evidence supports.

“Not located in the connected archive” is not “does not exist.” “Requested” is not “refused.” “Pending” is not “overdue” unless a controlling deadline and delivery record establish it.

REQUESTED

Sent and preserved

The scope, recipient, date, and delivery evidence are recorded.

PARTIAL

Some categories produced

Produced and omitted categories must be listed separately.

NOT LOCATED

Controlled archive status

The item has not been found in the systems actually searched.

WITHHELD / UNAVAILABLE

Basis must be stated

The custodian's actual explanation, legal basis, retention statement, or certification controls the description.

Evidence boundary: Nonproduction does not by itself prove that a record was destroyed, concealed, altered, or deliberately withheld.
WATERBURY POLICE

Complete incident file and controlling 2021 policies

The controlled archive includes a minimal incident report, dispatch material, EMS records, and the official-source 911 recording, but it does not presently contain a complete scene-documentation and audit trail.

Requested categories

  • Reports, supplements, officer notes, supervisor review, CAD attachments, radio records, scene photographs, video, diagrams, and evidence/property records.
  • Body-camera assignment, activation, audit, retention, deletion, and no-record explanations.
  • Policies governing sudden deaths, scene documentation, evidence preservation, OCME notification, reporting, retention, and supervision.

Current limits

  • No complete visual or narrative scene reconstruction is available in the controlled production.
  • The absence of footage does not establish a 2021 policy violation without the controlling policy and equipment records.
  • The latest custodian response must be read before labeling any category refused, destroyed, or never created.

WATERBURY FIRE / EMS

Complete medical-response and device record

The family archive does not contain a complete operational, clinical-device, QA, amendment, and communications record for the November 21 response.

Requested categories

  • CAD and radio records, complete crew narratives and amendments.
  • CPR documentation, AED or monitor event logs, rhythm strips, interventions, medications, and responder assignments.
  • Photographs or video, QA and supervisor review, and communications with police and OCME.

Processing boundary

  • Medical portions may require estate authority, proof of relationship, authorization, subpoena, or another lawful release path.
  • A documentation requirement is not automatically a denial.
  • The public site does not publish restricted medical-response details.
CONNECTICUT OCME

Complete inquiry, decision, and cremation-approval file

The controlled OCME item is a telephone notice/contact record. It is not a complete inquiry file or an autopsy report.

Requested categories

  • Referral, intake, call logs, investigator notes, jurisdiction decision, factual basis, and decision-maker.
  • Communications with police, EMS, clinicians, funeral home, crematory, and family.
  • Documents reviewed, release and cremation records, policies applied, amendments, metadata, and audit history.

Current limits

  • The controlled file does not document an OCME autopsy, toxicology, scene examination, or independent body examination.
  • Incoming “no trauma” and “nothing suspicious” statements are not OCME examination findings.
  • The completed approval, timestamp, approving official, and full inquiry remain proof targets.
