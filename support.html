RESPONSIBLE ADVOCACY

Public pressure must remain lawful and evidence-led.

The project seeks independent review and complete records, not harassment, vigilantism, or interference with witnesses and proceedings.

Appropriate actions

  • Share source-linked summaries with accurate context.
  • Refer qualified professionals.
  • Support lawful record acquisition and expert review.
  • Submit specific, sourced corrections.
  • Encourage institutional transparency through lawful channels.

Prohibited and harmful actions

  • Threats, harassment, impersonation, surveillance, or doxxing.
  • Contacting or coordinating witnesses without authorization and counsel direction.
  • Publishing private medical, financial, address, signature, account, or device information.
  • Calling a person a murderer, poisoner, thief, forger, or conspirator beyond the controlling record.
  • Interfering with courts, counsel, agencies, experts, or evidence custodians.
