CONTROLLED CONCLUSION

Homicide and poisoning are not established

The present controlled record does not establish who caused Gina's death or prove homicide, poisoning, a specific substance, route, dose, or responsible actor through competent forensic evidence.

The lack of autopsy and toxicology leaves material traumatic, toxicologic, accidental, and natural alternatives unresolved.
